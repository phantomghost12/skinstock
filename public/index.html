How do I find the CSS selector?

On the product page (in Chrome on your phone or computer), long-press or right-click the price and choose Inspect. In the panel that opens, the highlighted line is the HTML element showing the price. Look for a class="..." or id="..." on it — a class becomes .that-class and an id becomes #that-id. Paste that in. Hit Test to confirm it finds the right number before saving.
